# 🐄 smaXtec TruAdvice™ Herd Advisory & ROI Platform (Prototype)

An interactive commercial demonstration platform built to model **smaXtec bolus telematics**, early disease intervention workflows, and producer economic returns for large-scale Midwest dairy operations (1,200-cow Holstein simulation).

---

## 📌 Core Advisory Pillars Modeled

1. **Herd Health & 5-Day Early Detection:**
   - Pre-clinical inner body temperature (IBT) fever tracking ($\pm 0.018^\circ\text{C}$ sensor precision).
   - Identifies health lags $48\text{ to }120\text{ hours}$ before clinical signs to support non-antibiotic early intervention ($-70\%$ antibiotic target).

2. **Reproduction & Calving Prediction:**
   - Optimal insemination window detection via real-time activity index spikes.
   - Parturition countdown triggered by documented pre-calving reticular temperature drops ($\approx 15\text{ hours}$ in advance).

3. **Feeding Management & Rumen Health:**
   - Continuous reticular pH monitoring across ration groups to detect and mitigate Subacute Ruminal Acidosis ($\text{pH} < 5.8$ for $>3\text{h}$).

4. **Heat Stress & Barn Climate Mitigation:**
   - Barn Temperature-Humidity Index (THI) dynamic tracking correlated with animal water intake (TruDrinking) and rumination recovery.

5. **Interactive Producer ROI Calculator:**
   - Real-time financial modeling estimating bottom-line value across days open reduction, milk preservation, and veterinary savings.
